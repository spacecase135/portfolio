Place / Ritual / Transformation

Intentional Spaces

Rooms and landscapes reimagined as living compositions—shaped through art, ecology, function, and care to change how a place feels from the inside.

PROJECT 01 / WATER GARDEN

A sacred
space.

A neglected corner becomes a pond sanctuary organized around existing stone. The process moves from excavation and lining to planting, water, light, and a dense handmade ecology.

PROJECT 02 / THE STUDIO

The importance
of expression.

An basement becomes a generous creative room: instruments, artwork, work surfaces, and a hand-painted tropical mural turn blank utility into collective expression with creation in mind..

PROJECT 03 / DOMESTIC ALTARS

Everyday
orientation.

Personal expression through craftful decor alteration provide: a compass rose woodburned into an Oak table, an outdated window casing creatively reimagined.

PROJECT 04 / EXTERIOR RENEWAL

A brighter
welcome.

Exterior painting gives a local business a renewed presence. Updated window surrounds bring clarity, character, and care back to the building’s public face.
